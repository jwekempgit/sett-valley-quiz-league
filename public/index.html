League rules

£10per team, per season
6players at most
9pmThursday start
30 + 30questions a match

Teams and entry

  1. An entrance fee of £10 per team shall be paid at the start of the season. If at any time this does not seem sufficient, a further small sum may be levied.
  2. Competing teams shall consist of no more than six players per side. Teams cannot change players during a match and no player may join the team after the match has been playing for ten minutes.
  3. Teams that have no representative at the AGM, or have not contacted the organisers, will not be allowed to play in the following season.

Match night

  1. The matches shall be played on Thursday evenings starting at 9.00 p.m. except where both teams agree to a later start.
  2. Away teams are to collect that night’s questions from Chalkers at Newtown at 8:30 p.m.
  3. The questions shall be issued to the home team in sealed envelopes that will be opened by the question master in the presence of both teams. It is the responsibility of the home team to supply a question master. The away team may provide a scorer / timekeeper, if they wish.

The questions

  1. Sets of questions shall be provided and no others shall be used.
  2. No more than six questions can be on the same subject in any one match.

Playing the match

  1. The match shall consist of thirty questions to each team. The toss of a coin will decide whether a team wishes to go first or second.
  2. All questions to be answered jointly by the whole team. If no answer is given within thirty seconds, the timekeeper shall inform the team captain that their time is up, but still give them the opportunity to answer promptly.
  3. The question master will award one point for a correct answer. Should a wrong answer be given, or the team fail to answer, the question will be offered to the opposing team. The captain must answer within ten seconds and the question master will award one bonus point for a correct answer.

Disputed questions

  1. If there is a dispute to the validity of a question (i.e. the answer written down is considered incorrect):
    • If a team gives an answer that is the same as the supplied answer then that team may have the points no matter how incorrect the answer may seem.
    • A spare will be used if the question is not viable, i.e. unreadable, missing or the question person reads the answer out by mistake, etc.

Results and the table

  1. The home team of each match must send the result through to the organisers using the website. This also applies to Knockout/Plate matches. Send a score →
  2. If at the end of the season two teams have equal points, then to decide who is higher the two results of when they played each other is compared. If that is a draw then the number of points scored by each team when they played each other will be compared. If these are equal then the number of points scored during the season will be compared.

Join the league

The Sett Valley Quiz League has been running for many years and provides an opportunity for teams to compete in a challenging but friendly weekly quiz competition. Home venues range from Chapel-en-le-Frith to Disley, including Whaley Bridge, New Mills, Hayfield and Chinley. There are currently nine teams in the league (see map below).

While we are mainly looking to recruit entirely new teams and venues, interested individuals are welcome to make contact with the organisers at info@settvalleyquizleague.com, and we’ll put you in touch with existing teams who might welcome new blood.

Schedule and competitions

SVQL matches are held weekly on Thursday nights from September to April or May, starting at 9pm (or 8.30 by negotiation with the opposition) with gaps for Christmas and Easter. Each team plays each other home and away over the season to determine final league positions.

Venues

Teams need a home venue, which should have a bar and ideally a fairly quiet space where the quiz (which takes about an hour, with a half-time break of 10 minutes or so) can be held. Some venues provide light refreshments after the quiz, but this is not mandatory. As teams play home and away, each venue will only be required to host a fixture every other week on average.

Questions

All teams answer the same set of 60 general-knowledge questions, set by a team that isn’t playing that week; each team can expect to set the questions two or three times per season. The away team collects the sealed questions from a central venue on the night. Questions are accessible, but more challenging than the average fun quiz: think Mastermind general-knowledge round rather than Tipping Point.

Teams and format

A full team consists of six players, one of them acting as captain, plus a question-master. Squads can have more than six members, but only six can play on the night. Questions are asked alternately to each team, who have 30 seconds for discussion before the captain announces the team’s collective answer. If the answer is deemed wrong, the question is passed to the opposition, who have 10 seconds to answer.

Captains

As well as their matchday role, captains are also responsible for ensuring that, when it’s their team’s turn to set the quiz, the questions are delivered to the collection point on the night (although the captain does not have to be the person who sets the questions). Captains are also expected to attend the league AGM, to ensure that the league’s modest annual subscription (currently £10 per team and not required every season) is paid, to liaise with other team captains as necessary, and to pass on relevant information from the league organisers to their team members. It’s not an onerous role and can be shared.

Questionmaster

In each match, the home team is required to provide a question-master, who asks the questions and keeps score. Some teams have dedicated question-masters while others rotate the job around squad members. Once the final score has been communicated by the question-master and agreed by the two captains, it is the home captain’s responsibility to submit the score to the league website within 24 hours.

League setup

The league is run by an informal committee of three. Results and current league tables are published on this website. The league AGM is normally held a couple of weeks before the start of the new season.
